Problem

The obvious metric is not weakly powered — it is invalid

The natural test is: apply a condition's genetic edits, rank every metabolite by how much the model says it moved, and check where the measured product lands. Run on 24 real conditions it sits exactly at chance.

The instinct is to blame the sample size. That would be the wrong diagnosis. The metric fails for two structural reasons, and neither is fixed by more conditions.

LASER has no negatives on the metabolite axis

“They measured lycopene” is not evidence that nothing else moved — it is evidence of what the authors chose to assay. The dataset is a record of screening decisions, not a probing design, so there is no latent negative class to recover. No panel construction, matching scheme or normalisation creates one.

The model's response to an addition is close to guaranteed

Rayleigh monotonicity means two-terminal effective conductance rises unconditionally when conductance is added to a network. For the metabolite a design was built to feed, “it went up” therefore carries no information.

One nuance matters, because the whole replacement design rests on it: per-metabolite draw under ECSPr's universal-leak formulation is not monotone. Source injection is fixed, so metabolites compete for it, and opening a new sink can lower another metabolite's draw. The off-diagonal of the prediction is genuinely two-sided even though the diagonal is not.

Pivot

Hold the target fixed and vary the design

Negatives cannot come from what LASER measured. They can come from designs LASER did not build. That reframing also happens to ask the question a metabolic engineer actually has — which of these should I build? — rather than the one nobody asks.

The object everything reads

A design × target matrix: M[d,t] is the predicted change of design d at target t, over all designs and all resolvable targets, z-scored within column so a target's baseline reachability cancels. One measure_leak solve returns a draw for every metabolite, so a whole row costs one solve. FBA fills the identical matrix with Δflux.

Where the negatives come from

Three sources, and the design uses all three because they fail differently.

  • Synthesised counterfactual designs — the headline. Draw n_add reactions from the pooled LASER add universe and n_del from the deletion universe, size-matched to the real design. Unlimited n; controls monotonicity by construction, because the counterfactuals add the same number of edges.
  • Other papers' real designs — the off-diagonal. Stronger negatives, since somebody actually built and published them, but columns are badly imbalanced and edit-set size is uncontrolled. Free: no extra solves.
  • The same paper's alternative arms — the cleanest comparison possible, same lab and same assay. Measured out at 6 usable series. A case study, not a metric.

Of 175 source papers, only 23 contribute more than one arm. Requiring a shared target, comparable measurement type and distinct gene sets collapses that to six usable series. Reporting an aggregate Kendall τ over six papers would be theatre; the series are named individually instead.

Building the counterfactual pool

Sizes are drawn from the empirical joint (n_add, n_del) distribution of the real designs — heavy-tailed, median 5 total edits, mean 7.4, max 68 measured — so the pool covers the observed range by construction rather than by matching after the fact. Counterfactuals pass through the identical edit-application policy, so a counterfactual deletion of an absent reaction no-ops exactly as a real one does.

Exact matching on (n_add, n_del) is not affordable: the 235 designs occupy 35 distinct size cells even capped at 5, eight of them singletons. So matching is binned on total edit count crossed with a three-way composition class, and a size-regressed studentised residual is reported alongside — it uses all 500 counterfactuals for every condition and is far better powered where a bin is thin. If the two disagree materially, size dependence is doing the work and the report says so.

The trivial baseline runs through the identical counterfactual harness — its own pool of 500, its own BH-corrected significant fraction — so the arms are reported as a margin over it rather than in isolation. Without that, the benchmark measures whether you read the input.

Two findings that make the FBA arm non-trivial

  • Adding 117 open demand reactions to unmodified iML1515 raises maximum growth from 0.877 to 1.384 measured, because the drains unblock dead-end-limited reactions. Every panel demand is therefore pre-created at bounds (0,0) and a condition is realised by bound flips alone.
  • Inserting a lycopene condition's seven novel reactions irreversibly gives maximum production of 0.0 for every new metabolite; reversible with two bridges gives 0.959 measured. MetaNetX writes the carotenoid desaturases in the reductive direction. Reactions insert reversible by default, and that is a measurement, not a style choice.
Failure census, computable before a single LP runs

Your expectation that flux balance will simply break on some conditions is quantified: 30 of 235 conditions have no effective edit at all, 106 orphan metabolites span 85 of the 128 genuinely novel reactions, and 344 of 472 distinct add reactions are already native to the host. This is emitted as an ordered failure enum in the output schema, not as a log line.

Scoring

One headline number, three supporting reads

For each condition, take its predicted change at its own resolved target, locate the null distribution of that target's change over the counterfactual designs in its size bin, and compute an empirical p. Benjamini–Hochberg across 235.

The reported headline is the fraction significant at q < 0.05 against an expected 5% under the null, with a bootstrap CI resampled at the paper level — Record1420666912.41 contributes nine arms and they are not nine independent draws. The median empirical p accompanies it as a threshold-free summary. FBA produces the same number from Δflux, so the head-to-head is direct.

Two conventions are inherited rather than reinvented: empirical_p and bh_fdr come verbatim from examples/scadc_ecspr_null_score.py, including its (exceed+1)/(n+1) convention and 0.05 threshold.

Four states, not two

A metabolite absent from an arm's graph is a missing panel slot, not a failed prediction — folding it in as zero would let the arm with the smaller graph win by abstention. So ok enters every metric; below_floor enters ranking as tied-at-zero but is excluded from sign and correlation, because the sign of round-off is meaningless; created goes to Panel C only; and not_in_base leaves the denominator entirely.

The floor is real and it spans eleven orders of magnitude measured: jitter noise sits at 1.3e-14, a one-add/one-delete condition moves 1.8e-13, an eight-deletion condition moves 1.08e-3.

Execution

Five tasks, in dependency order

T1

Mapping layer and the coverage waterfall

Submodule init; bake orientation gate; resolve all 116 target strings and every carbon source to MetaNetX through a documented cascade with reviewer-attributed overrides; emit the edit universe and the seeded counterfactual pool. Ships the answer to “how much of LASER is scoreable at all”.

~½ day
T2

The ECSPr arms and the counterfactual pool

One in-process driver — not the CLI, which re-reads a 2.4M-row table per call. Three arms, directed and undirected, over 235 real plus 500 synthesised designs, with per-design shard checkpointing and a real log file.

4–5 h compute
T3

The flux balance arm

First FBA in this repo. Scaffold over the union of all 472 pooled add reactions so the counterfactual pool never triggers a model rebuild; per-record media; growth-coupled maximisation with an α sweep; ordered failure taxonomy and a --dry-run census.

~1½ h compute
T4

Scoring

The arm-agnostic contract, the counterfactual null, the off-diagonal test, strata as columns, paper-level bootstrap, and the per-bin size-distribution gate that separates a real null from a matching artefact.

~½ day
T5

Report

Six tables and seven figures. The coverage waterfall is the most important table and every figure caption cites its row; the per-condition forest of observed Δ against its counterfactual null band is the figure that shows which designs each method gets right.

~½ day

The counterfactual pool is generated once, seeded, recorded in the run manifest, and shared byte-identically by every ECSPr arm and by FBA. Regenerating it per arm would silently void every head-to-head comparison in the report. Total compute is 6–8 hours projected, dominated by the de-novo arms; it checkpoints per design, because this machine kills long jobs.

Risk

What would falsify this, and what a null would mean

The pilot says the headline may well come back null. The harness is built so that a null is a publishable result with an attribution attached, rather than a number with no explanation.

  • A matching artefact, not a result. If a size bin's real conditions sit at the top of its range against counterfactuals clustered at the bottom, significance is manufactured. Per-bin size distributions for real versus counterfactual designs are a gate, not an appendix.
  • The direction ensemble rather than the graph. The undirected arm runs alongside every directed one. Direction ratios span 3e-18 to 3.3e17 and the Hessian is near-singular; if undirected shows the same result, the finding is about the atom graph, not the ensemble.
  • The method only works where the answer is in the edit list. The stratification table answers this without a second run — 122 conditions with no heterologous add against 185 add-bearing ones, each with the trivial baseline's margin.
  • Two labelled “down” observations. Anything depending on separating up from down is a sanity check in this dataset, never a result. Stated in the report rather than discovered by a reader.
The result worth watching

The de-novo arms may be more interesting than the GEM arm. De-novo annotation sees 6,452 carbon metabolites against the GEM's 1,022, moving 50 targets and 118 observations into the scoreable panel against the GEM's 34 and 89. A method that has more chemistry to work with getting more of the benchmark is itself the FabFos claim — and it is testable here.
