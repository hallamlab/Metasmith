The invariant, before any of the numbers

Coverage cannot go down. It is a property of the construction.

A reaction the old measure admits keeps its expanded string byte for byte — same universe, same map, same pairs, same weights. The collapse is attempted only for a reaction that would otherwise have been refused. So there is no path by which an existing row moves, and the superset check below is a confirmation rather than a hope.

Why this scope exists

Every nitrogenase is back, and here they are by name.

Fifty-five reactions in the universe carry an N₂ species. Five of them were refused, and they are the five that write the actual nitrogen-fixation chemistry — while the acetylene-reduction assay, a small proxy for exactly that chemistry, sailed through the whole time. The bake's nitrogen story was missing precisely where the organism's is.

The threshold, re-derived rather than reused

The measure was wrong. The number turns out to be right.

600 was calibrated against the expanded count, and a curve stops describing a measure the moment you change what is being counted — so carrying the constant across would have been borrowing a justification. Taken the same way the original was: bin every buildable reaction by its collapsed atom count, join to the reactions the deployed bake actually banked, and cut where the yield falls off.

A debt the cap was already carrying

The deployed bake holds fourteen reactions the current cut refuses.

Found while building the curve, and it is not caused by this change — the 600-atom expanded cut already loses them, and has since it landed. The collapse repays three. The other eleven carry no stoichiometric repeats at all: their collapsed count equals their expanded one, between 603 and 961 atoms, so no amount of better counting reaches them.

They would need ATOM_LIMIT itself raised, which is a different decision with a different cost: the 600–800 expanded bin holds 117 reactions banking at 12.8%, so raising the line to 700 attempts roughly a hundred more reactions to recover about fifteen. Deliberately left alone here. Named in results/deployed_bake_debt.tsv.

The second half — what is still refused

Partial mapping, and what it can be expected to reach.

Seventy-eight reactions remain refused by size, and a much larger population banks nothing for a reason that is not chemistry at all: RXNMapper's transformer accepts 512 tokens, and the reactions it returns nothing for are the long ones — median SMILES length 983 against the universe's 268. A context-window limit, eating a biased sample.

That bias is what makes the fix sound. For a single element most of a long reaction is irrelevant — 3 NADPH + 3 NADP⁺ blows the limit while contributing no sulfur at all. So the submission keeps only the participants carrying that element, requires the element to balance across what remains, and reads only that element's pairs back out. Dropping a participant that carries no X cannot re-route an X atom, which is what separates this from the strip the extractor refuses.

Forced exactly means conservation leaves one possibility and no mapper is involved: one substrate and one product carrying the element in equal counts is a unique bijection. At the ≥ 900-character row — the closest local match to the recorded no-mapping set — that arm alone is 12,567 pair rows for zero mapper time.

The real target set cannot be computed here. It is admitted and still ended with nothing, which is a fact about a run, so the table sweeps reaction length rather than pretending to know where the token limit bites. The lane itself computes it properly, by subtracting every member's pairs table from what was admitted.

A partial map can never overwrite a real one. The layer goes down last, and the stack restricts each layer to what nothing below it claimed — so a partial contributes nothing at all where a member already reached, not a lower weight and not a second row. Every row it does contribute carries source=partial, and the ledger gains banked_partial and partial_declined so a partially-mapped reaction is never counted as banked or as dropped.

What none of this shows

Admitted is not banked.

Everything above measures which reactions the mappers are now allowed to see. Whether they produce pairs for them is what a rebake answers, and no local artifact stands in for it. The goal's own success indicator — that the Nostoc GPR builds a network whose AAM permits N fixation — needs the rebake and then the GPR, in that order. What can honestly be claimed today is the rung below it: the reactions carrying the chemistry are admitted, and their distinct chemistry is small enough that the mappers have every reason to reach them.

Yours to sign off

Two decisions, each with the numbers behind it.

Decision 1 — pair weights

Leave the collapsed reactions' weights as mapped?

n_atoms counts atom-index triples, so a collapsed reaction emits one triple per pair where an expanded one emits sixteen. No existing row moves — the collapse only touches reactions that were already refused — but the new rows sit on a different footing from a comparable expanded reaction's.

Sized: the recovered set carries a median multiplicity of 4.7× (IQR 3.4–6.6, max 89×), and at the deployed bake's 37 rows per reaction it is an estimated 17,021 rows, 0.7% of the table.

Recommendation — leave them as mapped Re-expanding by coefficient would make a reaction's edge weight scale with how many ATP it happens to hydrolyse, which is the same conflation of stoichiometry with chemistry that put the cap in the wrong place. At 0.7% of the table the choice is cheap to revisit; at 89× on the tail it is not cheap to get wrong.

Decision 2 — the rebake

Does this warrant the Sockeye time?

The collapse admits 454 reactions including all five nitrogenases, at no cost to any existing row. The partial lane adds a projected ~1,900 (reaction, element) combinations at the closest proxy, of which ~330 need no mapper at all. The whole R6 graph plans at 22 steps and MetaCyc is still its only given.

Against that: pass 3 is three more mapper lanes, and the reactions it targets are by construction the awkward ones. The forced arm is free and could ship alone.

Recommendation — rebake, once The five nitrogenases are the reason this scope exists and nothing short of a bake puts them in a table. Running the partial lane in the same pass costs one extra stage rather than a second campaign, and its ledger words mean a disappointing result is legible rather than invisible.
