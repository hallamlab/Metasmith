metasmith benchmark · objective 1 · E1 vs E2 on CAMI II

Does metasmith reproduce nf-core/mag?

E1 ran nf-core/mag 5.5.0 on 249 CAMI II samples: 208 short-read and 41 nanopore. E2 ran the same pipeline as a plan metasmith solved from a library of the same tool calls.

1 · workflow

The solved plan is nf-core's pipeline, step for step

Steps only: each data node is folded into the step that produces it. Left, nextflow's own DAG of the E1 run, cut to the processes that ran on that arm. Right, the plan metasmith solved for E2, which is the plan that ran. All four are drawn by metasmith's DAG renderer at the same scale, and a step's function has the same colour in each. This grid also leaves out the FastQC reports, and nf-core's seqkit statistics, FASTA splitting and depth summaries. The full graphs at the end of the page keep every step, with its data. The plan's two scoring steps against the CAMI gold standard are left out, because nf-core has no counterpart to them, and so is nf-core's Prodigal gene calling.

    2 · bin reproduction

    The same genomes, in the same numbers on short reads

    CheckM2 quality of every bin set, and how many of nf-core's bins metasmith reproduces. A bin is reproduced when it has a reciprocal partner in the other run: skani ANI ≥ 95 and aligned fraction ≥ 50% in both directions.

    Top: high-quality (solid) and medium-quality (hatched) bins in each bin set over all 249 samples, labelled with their sum. Bottom: the share of the first run's bins with a partner in the second. The black ticks are nf-core against itself. Two control reruns, E1ctl and E1ctl2, binned E1's own assemblies for 25 of the samples. E1ctl changed SemiBin2's seed, and E1ctl2 changed MetaBAT2's and SemiBin2's. Each tick is one of the three pairs among E1 and the two reruns, and the line spans them. The blue point is E1 against metasmith over all 249 samples.
