E5 hybrid pilot: graph resolution against polishing

E5 adopts MEGAHIT → OPERA-MS. It holds the most sequence in long contigs on CAMI's PacBio sets, is the cheapest hybrid lane on every CAMI dataset, and stays within 3 points of the best recovery everywhere.

CautionPratama, the one real Nanopore dataset, favours polishing: Flye + POLCA costs 45 CPU-hours against 88 for OPERA-MS, with an N50 of 26.7 kb against 4.3 kb. Re-test the choice when E5 runs Pratama at scale.

The lanes

QC
bbduk. CAMI uses JGI's settings, which keep 99.97% of CAMI II's bases. Pratama's own trimq=20 keeps 14%, because CAMI II simulates Q3 base calls.
MEGAHIT
Short reads only. The baseline for graph resolution.
OPERA-MS 0.9.0
Scaffolds and gap-fills MEGAHIT's contigs with the long reads. No reference clustering, no polishing.
Flye 2.9.6
Long reads only, --meta, in the platform's read mode. The baseline for polishing.
Flye + POLCA
Flye's contigs corrected with the short reads by POLCA from MaSuRCA 4.1.4.

The pilot draws 10% of the samples of every dataset with both long and short reads: CAMI II marine (1), plant PacBio (2), plant Nanopore (2), strain (10), CAMI III toy humangut (2) and Pratama 2026 (2). Each sample assembles all of its reads.

Why the numbers are believable

CAMI II's published scores are higher because they came from pooled co-assemblies. Four checks confirm the single-sample numbers:

  1. The gold-standard assembly of marine sample 3, scored the same way, has 0 mismatches, 0 indels and 0 misassemblies.
  2. Counting only uniquely mapped contigs moves marine's error rates by 10–30% and keeps every lane in the same order.
  3. One sample covers only 26–60% of its genomes, by its own gold standard. MEGAHIT reaches 29–35% of that ceiling on CAMI II, and CAMI II's pooled marine MEGAHIT reached 53% of its pooled ceiling.
  4. CAMI II also found Flye weak on marine and strong on plant Nanopore.

Joining Flye with MEGAHIT

flye --subassemblies keeps only the regions both assemblies share, 8–26% of its input, and recovery falls to 3–17%. A merge that adds the MEGAHIT contigs Flye + POLCA lacks has the best recovery on every scored dataset (23.7, 11.2, 13.9 and 40.0%). It loses long contigs on CAMI PacBio and costs 1.3–4.3× the OPERA-MS lane, so it does not change the decision.
